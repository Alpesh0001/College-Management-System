========================================
COLLEGE MANAGEMENT SYSTEM - USE CASES
========================================

This document outlines the specific Use Cases and features available for each of the 4 primary roles in the College Management System: Admin, HOD, Faculty, and Student.


1. ADMIN USE CASES
--------------------------------
The Admin has full control over the structural and administrative data of the college management system.

- Dashboard & Analytics: 
  * View overall statistics (total revenue, total students, total faculties, total courses)
  * View graphical or summarized reports on college activities.
- Course Management:
  * Add, Update, and Delete Courses (e.g., B.Tech, MCA).
  * Manage course details and duration.
- Subject Management:
  * Add, Update, and Delete Subjects for specific courses and semesters.
- Division/Class Management:
  * Create and Manage Divisions/Classes.
  * Assign students to divisions.
  * Assign Class Teachers to divisions.
- User Management:
  * Faculty Management: Add new Faculties and HODs, assign them to departments/courses, edit details.
  * Student Management: Handle new student admissions, generate IDs/Roll numbers, view and update student details.
- Fee Management:
  * Define fee structures.
  * View overall fee collections and pending payments.


2. HOD (HEAD OF DEPARTMENT) USE CASES
--------------------------------
The HOD role is an elevated faculty role with department-level administrative privileges, specifically related to scheduling.

- Timetable Management:
  * Create and Manage Timetables for the entire department/divisions.
  * Assign faculties to specific time slots and subjects.
- Faculty Level Privileges:
  * View departmental and assigned Timetables.
  * Add, Edit, and Delete Study Materials (PDFs, docs, images).
  * Add, Edit, and Delete Assignments for subjects they manage.
  * Mark and track Student Attendance.
  * View the list of students in their assigned divisions.
- Profile Management:
  * Update profile information and change the account password.


3. FACULTY USE CASES
--------------------------------
Faculties manage daily academic operations for the subjects and classes they are assigned to.

- View Timetable:
  * Check their assigned daily schedule and classes.
- Academic Materials:
  * Add, Edit, and Delete Study Materials for their assigned subjects.
- Assignment Management:
  * Create, Edit, and Delete Assignments with specific due dates.
  * View assignment submissions from students.
- Attendance Management:
  * Mark daily attendance for students in their lectures/classes.
  * View attendance history for their subjects.
- Student Management:
  * View details and lists of students in their assigned divisions.
- Profile Management:
  * Update personal information and change password.


4. STUDENT USE CASES
--------------------------------
Students use the app to stay updated on their academic schedule, materials, and administrative tasks.

- Academic Schedule:
  * View the daily/weekly Timetable for their specific division.
- Study Materials & Assignments:
  * View and download Study Materials uploaded by faculties.
  * View assigned Assignments, descriptions, and due dates.
- Attendance Tracking:
  * View their own subject-wise Attendance records and percentage.
- Fee Management:
  * View total fees, paid amounts, and pending dues.
  * Pay pending fees online securely via Razorpay.
  * Download/view fee payment receipts.
- Profile Management:
  * View personal admission and structural details.
  * Update contact information and change password.
